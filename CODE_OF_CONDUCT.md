# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, appearance,
ability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Protect candidate data and report security issues privately.
- Consider the impact of interview technology on marginalized groups.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
privacy violations, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the security
contact or a private GitHub channel. Maintainers may remove content, restrict
participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project.
