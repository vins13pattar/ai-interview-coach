# Five-Minute Reference Walkthrough

## 0:00–0:40 — Positioning

Explain that this is open coaching and decision-support software, not an
automated interviewer or hiring decision. Show the keyless deterministic mode
and BYOK boundary.

## 0:40–1:25 — Explicit workflow

Open the architecture diagram. Show the typed LangGraph nodes and explain that
models interpret evidence while deterministic code owns evidence sufficiency,
difficulty, follow-up, interruption, budgets, completion, and pronunciation
availability.

## 1:25–2:35 — Adaptive journey

Start a Principal Engineer text interview. Give a strong answer with a measured
outcome and show difficulty increase. Give a vague answer and show the targeted
evidence follow-up. Give a rambling answer and show the recorded natural-boundary
interruption decision.

## 2:35–3:15 — Durability and failure

Pause, reload, and resume without repeating a completed turn. Explain
idempotency, optimistic concurrency, PostgreSQL checkpoints, and explicit
provider-fallback provenance.

## 3:15–4:05 — Evaluation evidence

Show the 156-case computed baseline, including weak metrics. Show the 30-pair
counterfactual report and synthetic calibration demonstration. State clearly
what these reports do not validate.

## 4:05–4:40 — Voice and privacy

Choose voice mode and show consent before microphone access, raw-audio-off
policy, visible states, barge-in, and text fallback. Do not claim real-provider
latency unless the documented device matrix has been completed.

## 4:40–5:00 — Candidate agency

Complete the report, show uncertainty and rubric provenance, export the data,
then delete the session and checkpoints. Close with known limitations and the
remaining production gates.
