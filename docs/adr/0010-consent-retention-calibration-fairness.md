# ADR 0010: Consent, Retention, Calibration, and Fairness Evidence

Status: Accepted

Consent is versioned and purpose-specific. Raw audio retention is off;
transcripts support scoring/resume and remain candidate-exportable/deletable.
Synthetic datasets and counterfactual tests are labelled as engineering
evidence, never expert calibration or population fairness. Production claims
require qualified reviewers, privacy review, accessibility evidence, and
published limitations.
